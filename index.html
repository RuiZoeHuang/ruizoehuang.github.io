Chronic pain · Social inequality · Aging and the life course

I am Rui Huang, a medical sociologist and Postdoctoral Fellow at Seattle Children’s Research Institute. I received my Ph.D. in Sociology from the University at Buffalo. My research examines how social inequalities shape chronic pain and health over the life course. I study how early-life adversity influences later-life health, the resources that support resilience, and how welfare states and public policies can reduce health disparities. My work brings a sociopolitical perspective to understanding individual experiences of health and illness.

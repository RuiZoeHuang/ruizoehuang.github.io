My research examines chronic pain and social inequality over the life course, with attention to the institutional contexts that shape health.

## Chronic pain and social inequality

How do social and economic conditions shape who experiences pain and its consequences? My work examines disparities in pain across socioeconomic groups and places.

## Life course adversity and resilience

How do early experiences influence health in later life, and when can disadvantage be weakened? I study early-life adversity, later-life pain, and the resources that support resilience.

## Welfare states and public policy

How do institutional contexts shape health inequalities? My research considers welfare states across countries and public policies across U.S. states.

## Data and methods

I use population surveys and longitudinal data, drawing on research in the United States, Europe, and Vietnam.
